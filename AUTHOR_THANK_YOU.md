# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Mengda Li, Zeng Li, and Jianfeng Yao for making the mean-shift
contamination problem and the knockoff-mean PCA idea available as a concrete
reproduction target. The paper's combination of random-matrix analysis and a
simple perturbation procedure makes its assumptions, implementation choices,
controls, and failure modes unusually inspectable.

This repository is an independent audit and documentation effort. The authors
are not responsible for its code, finite checks, counterexamples, limitations,
status labels, or release decisions.
